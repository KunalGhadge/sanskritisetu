Stone Chariot AR

Place the Stone Chariot of Vittala Temple in your real environment using ARCore plane detection.

How it works

1
Tap the button — Google Scene Viewer opens with your camera
2
Slowly move your phone to scan your floor or table
3
Tap the detected surface — the chariot appears in real space!
4
Walk around it, change scale, take photos 📸

Requires Google Play Services for AR (ARCore).
If it doesn't open, install it from the Play Store.
